# Protect Your Home: Air Quality & Pest Risk

Recent headlines about hantavirus on a cruise ship serve as a stark reminder: rodents aren’t just a nuisance; they pose a significant threat to our health and safety. The AP story details how rodent droppings and urine can contaminate indoor air, leading to serious respiratory illness. This isn’t just a problem for cruise ships – it’s a risk present in homes and businesses across the Kansas City metro area.

Why does this matter? Rodents thrive in dark, undisturbed spaces – attics, basements, and crawlspaces are prime real estate for infestations. These areas often harbor significant amounts of rodent feces and urine, which, when disturbed, release airborne particles containing pathogens. These particles can trigger allergic reactions, asthma, and even serious infections. Maintaining good indoor air quality starts with proactively managing pest populations.
